No cookies

In its current version this site sets no cookies on your device. It uses no Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no session recording, no embedded map and no embedded video.

The typefaces are hosted on the same server as the site: your browser contacts no third-party service to display this page. No consent banner is therefore required.

What the server records

The host keeps technical logs (IP address, date, page requested) for a short period, for security and availability. These logs are not used for advertising tracking or behavioural analysis.

If a tool is added later

Adding analytics, a map or a pixel would trigger the obligation to inform you and obtain your prior consent, with refusal as easy as acceptance and the ability to withdraw at any time. Nothing of the kind will be enabled without that compliance work.
