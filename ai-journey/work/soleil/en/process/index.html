01

Process

How a project runs

The stages of a project, from the first meeting to handover.

The client’s actual process must replace this draft: no step may be invented.

Consultation

A free meeting on site: intended use, building constraints, budget frame. We say plainly what is feasible, and in what order.

Planning and proposal

Survey of the existing structure, scope, materials, a realistic schedule. A detailed line-by-line proposal, with no hidden amounts.

Preparation

Borough permits, site protection, material orders, trade coordination before the first hammer falls.

Construction

Execution, regular reporting, a site kept clean. One point of contact from start to finish.

Completion

Joint review, deficiency list, corrections, then handover with documentation and applicable warranties.
